Cybersecurity

Cybersecurity is the practice of protecting computers, networks, applications, and data from unauthorized access and cyberattacks.

Common threats include malware, phishing, ransomware, denial-of-service attacks, and SQL injection.

Authentication verifies the identity of a user, while authorization determines which resources the user can access.

Encryption protects sensitive information by converting readable data into an encoded format.

Firewalls, intrusion detection systems, multi-factor authentication, and regular security updates help protect digital systems.
